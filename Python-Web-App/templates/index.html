All About Sugar Gliders

Sugar gliders (Petaurus breviceps) are small, nocturnal gliding possums native to eastern and northern mainland Australia, New Guinea, and certain Indonesian islands. They get their common name from their preference for sugary nectarous foods and their ability to glide through the air, much like a flying squirrel!
